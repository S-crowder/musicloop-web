Moving songs from your laptop to your phone
  1. On the laptop, open a song in the Library (or a setlist) and tap Send to phone. It saves a .zip file.
  2. Plug the phone into the laptop, choose File transfer on the phone, and drag the .zip into the phone's Download folder. (Or use Quick Share, Google Drive or email.)
  3. On the phone, tap Choose stem files or zips above and pick it.

Logged in on both? Your library syncs by itself through your Musicloop server.

Linking your laptop

This copy runs inside Claude, which can't reach your Musicloop server, so it can't log in. To link your laptop and phone, start the Musicloop server and open its address in your laptop's browser, for example http://localhost:8000. Log in there and in the phone app with the same account.
